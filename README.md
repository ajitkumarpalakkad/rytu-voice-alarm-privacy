# Privacy Policy for Rytu Voice Alarm

**Last updated: 19 October 2026**

Rytu Voice Alarm is an alarm clock that wakes you with your own recorded
voice. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Short version

Rytu Voice Alarm does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.
Everything stays on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, and the
app does not need an internet connection to work.

## What the app stores

- **Your recordings.** Audio is recorded only while you hold or press the
  Record button. Recordings are saved in the app's own private storage on
  your device.
- **Your alarm settings.** Times, repeat days, titles, vibration and volume
  preferences are saved only on your device.

Both are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## What the app sends

Nothing. No recordings, no settings and no usage data leave your device.
The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind,
and makes no network requests.

## Permissions and why they are needed

- **Microphone** — to record your voice for an alarm. Used only while you
  are recording.
- **Audio files** — only if you choose to import an existing voice note
  (for example from WhatsApp). The app reads the one file you select.
- **Alarms and reminders, notifications, display over other apps, run at
  startup** — so the alarm can reliably ring and show its screen at the
  time you set, including after the phone restarts.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page
